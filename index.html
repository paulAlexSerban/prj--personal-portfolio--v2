More About Me

I build enterprise-grade software, driving the entire lifecycle from initial system design and architectural planning to deployed, scalable solutions. My engineering focus centers on Domain-Driven Design (DDD), CQRS, and building robust microservices and micro-frontend ecosystems.

Whether optimizing AWS cloud infrastructure, containerizing applications with Docker and Kubernetes, or architecting complex integrations, I prioritize measurable outcomes. I have a track record of leading development teams to deliver platforms that significantly reduce time-to-market and cut operational overhead.

Beyond the code, I am an experienced technical coach and team lead. I thrive in collaborative, Agile environments-frequently acting as a solution architect to untangle complex requirements, manage trade-offs (like navigating the CAP theorem), and guide teams to deliver high-quality products under tight constraints.

I am driven by systems thinking in both software and process. I actively explore architectural design patterns and knowledge management techniques, building workflows that streamline development, enforce high technical standards, and ensure continuous team growth.
