More About Me

I am a passionate Software Engineer with a specialized focus on Front-End Development. My expertise spans the entire web development process, from conceptualization and architectural planning to system design, full-stack coding, and troubleshooting.

With hands-on experience in JavaScript, React.js, Webpack, and Node.js, I excel at creating seamless, responsive, and component-driven web solutions. I have a proven track record in architecting, building, and refining user-centric front-end designs, utilising the latest front-end approaches and UI libraries such as Tailwind, Bootstrap, and UIKit.

Being focused on front-end, my specialisation extends to web accessibility and web performance, ensuring all projects adhere to the highest web standards through strategic optimisations and adhering to high web standards.

My skills extend beyond coding: I am adept at employing Agile methodologies and Scrum practices, fostering positive and productive team environments through open communication. As a DevOps enthusiast, I am proficient with AWS, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines, specifically with GitHub Actions, ensuring streamlined Software Development Life Cycles and efficient, scalable, and secure applications.

I am a continuous learner, committed to staying updated with the latest industry trends and deepening my understanding of system design with an aim to evolve into a Software Architect role.
